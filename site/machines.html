What has been tested on real hardware. I've tested LienCAM on my own router, which runs UCCNC with an AXBB-E controller. I don't own a GRBL, grblHAL or LinuxCNC machine, so those routes have only been tested against simulators (grbl-sim and the LinuxCNC simulator), not on real hardware yet. If you run one of those machines, your test reports are exactly what we need - and once people have confirmed them on real machines, this note comes off.

Post dialects

LienCAM posts G-code in five dialects:

  • UCCNC
  • GRBL
  • grblHAL
  • LinuxCNC
  • Generic
