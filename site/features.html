Features

One program, one workspace for each step from the first line to the finished cut. Each section links to its full guide in the wiki.

Sketch

  • Draw lines, arcs, circles and splines on any plane.
  • Constrain the drawing and add dimensions so the part stays the size you meant.
  • Add text and reuse shapes as blocks.
  • Import SVG and DXF drawings and start from them.

Model

  • Extrude, revolve, sweep and loft sketches into solids.
  • Fillet, chamfer, shell, draft and drill holes, with the feature tree open beside you.
  • Combine solids with boolean cuts and joins.
  • Import STEP, IGES and STL parts; export STEP and STL.

  • Watch the finished smooth body update as you work.

Toolpaths 2.5D

Ten operation types for flat and profile work. Each name below is the one the operation picker shows.

2D Face
Surfaces the top of the stock. Passes run past the edges so the cutter fully clears it. Zigzag or one-way passes.
2D Contour
Cuts along a profile at one or more depths. Choose from four cutting strategies, open or closed paths, holding tabs, ramped entry, spring passes, trochoidal roughing, and corner relief (dogbone or T-bone).
2D Pocket
Clears a closed region in rings and leaves islands standing. Enters by helix, ramp or plunge, finishes the wall, and offers corner relief and a slower slot feed.
2D Adaptive Clearing
Roughs a region while holding the side load on the tool near the value you set. Stay-down options keep the cutter down between passes, and a floor finish pass is available.
Drill
Drills at the centre of each picked or detected hole. Four cycles: simple, peck, chip break and dwell, with a depth for every hole.
Bore
Mills holes larger than the tool in circles, with roughing orbits, finish and spring passes.
Thread Mill
Cuts internal or external threads with a helix. Metric, UNC and UNF size presets, left-hand threads, and a cylinder pick that fills in the thread.
Engrave / Trace
Rides the tool along open or closed curves at a depth you set, or at a depth worked out from a line width. A trochoidal style cuts a slot wider than the tool.
V-Carve
Follows the centre line of letters and shapes with a V-bit, deeper where the outline is wide, with optional flat-floor clearing by a separate end mill.
2D Chamfer
Runs a V-bit or chamfer mill along profile edges and cuts a bevel to the depth you set, inside or outside.
  • Feeds and speeds come from your tool library, and you can override them for one operation.
  • Set the heights for each operation, and see its toolpath over the part.
  • Run any operation in the simulator before you cut.
  • Post every operation to your controller's G-code dialect.

Toolpaths 3D

Six operation types for shaped parts: one to rough and five to finish. Alpha: early, with limited proof.

Adaptive Rough (3D)
Clears stock level by level with light adaptive passes, down to the shape of the part. Set the stepdown, the entry helix and what to clear.
Parallel Finish (3D)
Lays parallel passes over the surface of a solid part, at the angle and stepover you choose, and can clip to the stock outline.
Waterline Finish (3D)
Follows the part at one height, steps down, and follows it again. A finish for steep walls, with a steep-areas-only choice.
Scallop Finish (3D)
Spaces its passes along the surface to hold the scallop height you set, so steep areas get passes closer together than flat ones.
Steep and Shallow (3D)
Finishes a part in one operation: waterline levels on the steep walls, parallel passes on the flatter areas, split at a threshold angle you set.
Pencil Cleanup (3D)
Runs along the inside corners of a part to clean out the material a larger finishing tool could not reach.
  • Feeds and speeds come from your tool library, and you can override them for one operation.
  • Set the heights for each operation, and see its toolpath over the part.
  • Run any operation in the simulator before you cut.
  • Post every operation to your controller's G-code dialect.

Post Process
