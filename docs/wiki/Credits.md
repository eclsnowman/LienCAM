# Credits

LienCAM exists because a great many people chose to share their work. Every project, library, font, model, algorithm, paper and published resource LienCAM stands on made some part of it possible. Thank you, all of you.

## The full lists

- [CREDITS.md](../blob/main/CREDITS.md) names each project, what it does in LienCAM, how it ships and a word of thanks. It is generated from `src/about/credits.json`.
- [THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md](../blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md) holds the licence terms and full licence texts for everything that ships with LienCAM.

## What LienCAM stands on

- **Geometry and CAD kernels.** The solid, mesh, sketch and polygon engines underneath every model and every toolpath, including Open CASCADE Technology and occt-wasm. See [Geometry kernel build](Geometry-Kernel-Build).
- **CAM algorithms and code we ported or adapted.** Toolpath strategies other people worked out first and published so others could build on them.
- **Rendering, UI and app platform.** What draws the screen, holds the state, runs the models and turns a web app into a desktop app.
- **Fonts and data.** The typefaces in the interface and the letters LienCAM can cut.
- **Machine control, standards and references.** The controllers, standards, papers and published specifications that keep LienCAM honest about real machines.
- **Tools we built it with.** Not shipped with LienCAM, but it could not have been built without them.

If you think something is missing or credited wrongly, please say so in [a discussion](../discussions) or [an issue](../issues).
