# B-rep kernel STEP test fixtures

Canonical STEP fixtures for the B-rep model kernel. Units mm, Z up, every
solid sits on the Z=0 plane. Generated by the adjacent Python + OCP generators
(for example `generate_smart_height_prismatic_fixture.py` and
`generate_feature_block_fixture.py`; STEP schema AP203 via STEPControl_Writer,
write.step.unit = MM).

Counts below were measured by reading each file back with STEPControl_Reader
and counting unique subshapes via TopExp_Explorer (deduped with
TopTools_MapOfShape). Use them as smoke-test constants.

## fix_block_pocket.step

- Block 80 x 60 x 20 (x 0..80, y 0..60, z 0..20).
- Open rectangular pocket in the top face: 50 x 30, depth 8, centered at
  (40, 30), corner radius 6. Pocket footprint x 15..65, y 15..45; floor at
  z = 12.
- Island inside the pocket: 12 x 8 x 8, centered at (40, 30)
  (x 34..46, y 26..34, z 12..20). Island top is flush with the block top.
- Construction: box cut by a corner-filleted box (fillet r6 on the 4
  vertical cutter edges), then fused with the island box.
- Counts: FACES 20, EDGES 48, SOLIDS 1.
  - Face breakdown: block 6 (top face carries the pocket-opening inner
    wire) + 4 planar pocket walls + 4 quarter-cylinder corner fillets
    + 1 pocket floor (carries the island inner wire) + 4 island sides
    + 1 island top.

## fix_plate_holes.step

- Plate 100 x 60 x 10 (x 0..100, y 0..60, z 0..10).
- 6 through holes (axes +Z):
  - d6  at (20, 20), (50, 20), (80, 20)
  - d10 at (30, 40), (70, 40)
  - d3  at (90, 40)
- Counts: FACES 12, EDGES 30, SOLIDS 1.
  - 6 plate faces + 6 cylindrical hole walls; per hole 2 circles + 1 seam
    edge (6 x 3 = 18 hole edges + 12 plate edges).

## fix_boss_taper.step

- Base block 60 x 60 x 10 (x 0..60, y 0..60, z 0..10).
- Centered tapered boss (truncated cone / frustum) fused on top: bottom
  diameter 30, top diameter 18, height 12. Axis at (30, 30), boss spans
  z 10..22.
- Counts: FACES 8, EDGES 15, SOLIDS 1.
  - 6 base faces (top face carries the circular inner wire) + 1 conical
    lateral face + 1 planar boss top. Edges: base 12 + base circle + cone
  seam + top circle.

## fix_feature_block.step

- Compact 60 x 40 x 20 block (x 0..60, y 0..40, z 0..20) with a left-open
  pocket (floor z=8) whose far wall is a single 15-degree drafted plane
  (floor edge at x=28, deck opening grown to x~=31.215), d6 through bore at
  (49, 9), d6 blind bore at (49, 31, floor z=10), a spherical cap r7 centered
  at (42, 20, 20), and a standing boss (x 51..59, y 15..25, z 20..25) with
  vertical walls and a flat top.
- The procedural benchmark twin (`featureBlock()` in
  `src/cam3d/fixtures/index.ts`) tessellates these SAME analytic faces --
  the drafted wall and the boss are both planar in this STEP twin too, so
  the two imports diverge only in the sphere's tessellation, not in shape.
- Counts: FACES 19, EDGES 45, SOLIDS 1 (read back 2026-09-05 with
  STEPControl_Reader and TopExp_Explorer, after the draft/boss fix round).

## fix_freeform.step

- Block 40 x 40 x 10 (x 0..40, y 0..40, z 0..10) fused with a sphere of
  radius 12 centered at (20, 20, 12) (tangent to Z=0 internally, bulge
  apex at z = 24, intersection circle in the top face radius ~11.83).
- Purpose: provides at least one NON-PLANAR, NON-CYLINDRICAL face
  (spherical) for planarity-rejection tests.
- Counts: FACES 7, EDGES 15, SOLIDS 1.
  - 6 block faces (top face carries the circular inner wire) + 1 spherical
    cap face. Edge count includes the sphere's seam edge and the degenerate
    pole edge (OCC sphere parameterization), so edges = block 12
  + intersection circle + seam + pole = 15.

## fix_prismatic_boss.step

- Base block 60 x 60 x 10 (x 0..60, y 0..60, z 0..10).
- Centered cylindrical boss, diameter 16, from z=10 to z=20.
- One fused solid with one exact lower deck and one fully nested higher region.
- Purpose: accepted reference fixture for the Smart Height prismatic target
  classifier. Regenerate with `generate_smart_height_prismatic_fixture.py`.
